The two crossovers to memorise

Sonnet 5 at Extra ($0.405) costs more than Opus 5 at High ($0.250) — and Opus is the stronger model.

Sonnet 5 at Max ($0.930) costs more than Opus 5 at Extra ($0.675).

Past Sonnet/High, change model, not effort. That is exactly the jump bkit already makes — Sonnet/medium straight to Opus/high, with nothing in between.

Going up a model tier costs 1.67× (Sonnet→Opus) or 2× (Opus→Fable). Going High→Max costs ~6×.

What bkit actually uses — 5 pairings

Haiku 4.5 · low the five *-summary agents

Sonnet 5 · medium the whole execution tier (~17 agents)

Opus 5 · medium plan-review-2, plan-scope-check-1

Opus 5 · high review, verify, audit, ship

Opus 5 · xhigh generate, update, diagnose, reconcile

Never max. Zero of 48 agents. The split is decide vs do: pay for the decision, economise on the execution.

Frontmatter effort overrides the session slider — so bkit is unaffected by wherever you leave it. Only CLAUDE_CODE_EFFORT_LEVEL outranks it; leave that unset.

Fine print

* Output-token figures are an estimate — Anthropic publishes no per-level token counts (effort is “a behavioral signal, not a strict token budget”). Costs are exact arithmetic on that estimate, at 10,000 input tokens per call.

† Cross-model rows are a cost ruler, not a capability equivalence: “the effort scale is calibrated per model, so the same level name does not represent the same underlying value across models.” Within a model the ordering is sound.

² Ultracode cost assumes a 15-agent workflow. Hard caps: ~16 concurrent, 1000 per workflow lifetime.

Ultracode: session-only. Enable with /effort ultracode, claude --effort ultracode (needs v2.1.203+), or the keyword “ultracode” in a prompt for one turn. Silently degrades to plain xhigh if workflows are off.

Thinking is a separate toggle from Effort. On Opus 5, keep Thinking on and lower Effort rather than switching it off — thinking-on at Low beats thinking-off at similar cost.
