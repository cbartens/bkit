# Design Brainstorm & Ideas

Lens: Architecture & orchestration shape — workflow scaffolding, agent topology, output-shape contracts, fit with `bkit-design`/`bkit-plan` pattern.

## Approach A — Faithful 7-step mirror of `bkit-design` skeleton, 12 sibling lens prompts ⭐ Recommended

**Summary:** One workflow file `bkit-audit.md` modelled byte-for-byte on `bkit-design.md` § Step 1–2 and § Failure Path; phase-specific steps slim to 5 (lens fan-out, synthesize, prioritize, summary, close); 12 sibling lens prompts `bkit-audit-lens-{slug}.md` each forked from `bkit-design-brainstorm-ideate-1.md` (Read-only-reviewer tool pattern: `Read, Grep, Glob, Write`).

Step 3 — parallel fan-out of 12 lens subagents (mirrors `bkit-design.md` § Step 3 §191–227 triplet pattern, scaled to N=12). Step 4 — synthesize (mirrors § Step 4 Generate Design). ## Approach B — Collapsed 5-step workflow with merged synthesize-prioritize-summary stage

**Summary:** Same 12-lens fan-out as A, but Steps 4–6 collapse to a single `bkit-audit-report` subagent that dedupes + prioritises + emits the final report in one pass; mirrors `bkit-plan.md` § Step 10 Summary's "highest version + status read + glob inputs" pattern but does the work in one xhigh agent.

| Aspect                 | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Workflow shape**     | Step 1 Start, Step 2 Worktree, Step 3 Lens fan-out (12 parallel), Step 4 Audit Report (single xhigh opus agent reads all 12 lens files + emits matrix + ranked list + per-class appendix + verdict), Step 5 Close. 5 steps total vs A's 7.                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Lens prompts**       | Identical to A.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Synthesis pipeline** | One `bkit-audit-report.md` = hybrid of `bkit-design-generate` (dedup) + `bkit-design-organise` (structural verify) + `bkit-design-summary` (Linear-bound verdict). Effort `max` (the only candidate currently for the `max` tier per `.agents/prompts.md` § Effort tiers — first user).                                                                                                                  |
| **Failure Path**       | Same as A.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Smoke test**         | Verdict criterion baked into `bkit-audit-report.md`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Complexity**         | LOW for workflow file (~150 fewer lines than A); HIGH for the single mega-prompt — it now owns dedup logic + matrix render + ranking + verdict + 500-token Linear summary, four jobs in one agent. Violates Stubbed-output single-responsibility convention.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Extensibility**      | MEDIUM — adding a new output dimension (e.g. confidence histogram) touches one large agent vs a small focused one. Adding a lens still trivial.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Risk**               | Truncation on the mega-agent — 12 lens files in, full report out, Linear-paste content out — could exceed opus 200K input or output budget on a debt-rich codebase. `.agents/prompts.md` § Models context-window note flags this exact concern. Also defeats the per-step verdict-recovery pattern (`design-organise` FAIL routes to Failure Path; merged step has no equivalent fine-grained recovery). |
| **Maintenance**        | MEDIUM — fewer files but mega-prompt is hard to evolve safely; any change risks breaking three concerns at once.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Reuse vs new**       | ~70% reuse — workflow is leaner but the mega-prompt is mostly net-new (no existing single-agent precedent for synthesize+prioritize+summarize). Worse template fit than A.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

## Approach C — 9-step workflow with explicit review/apply round on synthesized findings

**Summary:** Expansion of A — insert a `bkit-audit-review` parallel pair (mirrors `bkit-design.md` § Step 5 Design Review pair) between synthesize and prioritize, plus `bkit-audit-update` apply-review step (mirrors § Step 6). Treats the synthesized findings list itself as adversarially reviewable before ranking.

| Aspect                 | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Workflow shape**     | Steps 1–3 same as A; Step 4 synthesize → Step 5 review-pair (`bkit-audit-review-1`, `-2` — find missed findings, false positives, severity drift) → Step 6 apply review (`bkit-audit-update`) → Step 7 prioritize → Step 8 summary → Step 9 close. 9 steps.                                |
| **Lens prompts**       | Identical to A.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Synthesis pipeline** | Adds review-pair (clone of `bkit-design-review-1/2` minus WebFetch/WebSearch — audit findings don't need ecosystem search) and `bkit-audit-update` (clone of `bkit-design-update.md` — copy-prepend-verdict pattern, max-wins on CRITICAL).                                                |
| **Failure Path**       | Same as A; review-pair FAIL routes to Failure Path.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Smoke test**         | Could move smoke check into review-1's lens — explicit "did core-debt-class findings surface?" probe. Stronger guarantee than A but couples acceptance to runtime mechanism.                                                                                                               |
| **Complexity**         | HIGH — 9 steps, 18 prompts (12 lens + synthesize + 2 reviewers + update + prioritize + summary). Closest structural fit to `bkit-design` but heaviest.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Extensibility**      | HIGH — every stage independently evolvable; review pair generalises to other audit dimensions.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Risk**               | Over-engineered for read-only workflow with no PR. `bkit-design`'s review stage exists because design errors propagate to plan + build downstream; audit is terminal — findings either get acted on by a human or ignored. Review stage adds 2 opus runs + 1 xhigh run for marginal value. |
| **Maintenance**        | MEDIUM-HIGH — more sync points across prompts when changing the finding schema.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Reuse vs new**       | ~90% reuse from `bkit-design` but adds prompts that may never earn their token cost.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

---

**Lens summary:** This brainstorm focused on architecture and orchestration shape — how the new `bkit-audit` workflow should structure its steps, fan out its 12 lenses, contract its output schema, handle Failure Path semantics for a PR-less read-only flow, and bake in the smoke-test acceptance. Three approaches contrasted: (A) a faithful 7-step mirror of `bkit-design` with 12 sibling lens prompts and a synthesize/prioritize/summary pipeline — the recommended shape, maximising reuse of verbatim orchestrator ceremony and the established verdict-stub + Cross-workflow-alignment patterns; (B) a leaner 5-step collapse merging synthesize+prioritize+summary into one mega-agent — minimises workflow surface at the cost of single-responsibility, truncation safety, and template fit; (C) a heavier 9-step expansion adding an adversarial review-and-apply round over the synthesised findings — closest structural twin to `bkit-design` but over-engineered for a terminal read-only workflow with no downstream propagation. Decisions table locks in the structural choices already supported by the brief (full ceremony mirror, 12 sibling prompts, Stubbed-output + verdict-line discipline, organise-style matrix render, `docs/audits/{FID}-audit.md` output) and surfaces the open trade-offs the synthesiser must adjudicate (step count granularity, single-vs-mega synthesis agent, smoke-test runtime vs build-time).
